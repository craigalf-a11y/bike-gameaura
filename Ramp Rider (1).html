Ramp Rider

Hold Gas to build speed, then launch off the ramps. Hold the back arrow on the ground to ride a mono for points. Lean in the air so your wheels meet the slope. Miss the gap and you crash.

Level 1

Keyboard: Right arrow or D for gas, Left arrow or A for a mono (lift the front wheel), Down arrow or S to brake. In the air, hold Right for a frontflip or Left for a backflip, then let go to stop spinning and line up with the landing slope. Double and triple flips need a big jump and a fast run-up. Gas and a mono together at high speed loops you out, so ease off the gas. You can play with Space alone: hold it to ride, and keep it held (or tap it) in the air for a 360 spin (land it before the spin is half done, or you crash sideways). The track winds through left and right turns, so just keep your speed up. The bike levels itself in the air when you don't use the arrows. Space or Enter also starts a ride. Clear every jump and cross the finish gate to unlock the next level. Each level is harder.
